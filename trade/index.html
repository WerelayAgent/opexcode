Before you trade

Understand what you're trading.

Read these disclosures before using OpexCode.

  1. Third-party tokens

    xStocks are tokenized securities issued by a third party. They are not direct ownership of the underlying shares. Issuer terms and transfer restrictions apply.

  2. Trading involves risk

    Prices and liquidity can change, and you can lose funds. In Quick, review expected output, minimum received, slippage and fees. In OPmode, review exact input, output, fee and expiry when a quote is available. Liquidity providers hold pool shares and receive the pool's current asset composition when withdrawing, with possible gains or losses and no fixed yield.

  3. Your signature authorizes real funds

    Trading uses real wallet funds and requires your transaction signature. Network fees may apply even if a transaction fails. Signing in proves wallet ownership; it does not authorize a swap.

  4. Dollar pricing assumption

    Stock-referenced OPmode prices treat 1 USDC as $1. This is a pricing assumption, not a guarantee of USDC's market value. New quotes may pause if USDC moves outside the supported parity range.

  5. Check the outcome before retrying

    A delayed response can leave a submitted transaction pending. Check the saved order's status before creating a replacement.

Trading eligibility and country restrictions are checked separately. Acknowledging this notice does not establish eligibility.
